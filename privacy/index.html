Privacy policy

Your voyage is yours. Here’s how information is handled in Stay Afloat and on this website.

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Stay Afloat is developed by Tomás Ruiz López. For privacy questions or requests, email truizlop@gmail.com. This policy covers the game and this website; features may vary between development, beta and released versions.

Your voyage, on your device

Saved voyages, progress, discoveries and settings are stored locally. Stay Afloat does not operate its own account or cloud-save service. Your device’s backup settings may include app data. Deleting the app removes its local data; backups and records held by Apple or other providers are managed separately.

Purchases

If you use ad-free purchases, Apple processes payment and RevenueCat helps validate purchases, restore access and manage your entitlement. RevenueCat processes purchase history and a generated app user identifier, with technical information needed to provide its service. Stay Afloat does not receive your payment-card details. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy and Apple’s privacy policy.

Advertising and your choices

Ad-supported versions use Google AdMob. Google’s advertising SDK may process IP addresses and approximate location derived from them, device or app identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostic and performance information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and improve its services. Data use depends on configuration, permissions and your choices.

Google’s User Messaging Platform presents privacy choices where required. When available, revisit them in Settings → Ad privacy choices in the game. The app does not itself request App Tracking Transparency permission or read your advertising identifier. See Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses information from partner apps.

Game Center

If you sign in, Apple provides your Game Center identifier and display name so the game can show your profile. Online score and achievement reporting is not enabled in the current version. Apple handles Game Center information according to its Game Center privacy notice. You can manage sharing and sign-in in your device’s Game Center settings.

This website and support

This website uses GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security under its privacy statement. We do not add advertising cookies, analytics trackers or third-party video embeds to this site. Gameplay clips are served directly with the website.

If you email us, we receive your email address and anything you include, and use it to answer your request. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information.

Purposes and retention

Information is used to provide the features you request, maintain purchases, respond to support and keep services secure. Where required, advertising processing relies on your consent; other processing may be necessary to provide a service, meet legal obligations or pursue legitimate interests such as security and support.

Support correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle the request and related legal obligations. Providers retain information according to their policies and may process it outside your country using applicable safeguards.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your information, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent. Contact truizlop@gmail.com; you may also complain to your local data protection authority. We cannot directly access or delete information stored only on your device or independently controlled by Apple or Google.

Families and changes

Parents or guardians with questions about a child’s information can contact us. Apple’s purchase and Game Center parental controls are available through device settings. We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date above.
