01 / LIFE ON BOARD

They’re on holiday. You’re not.

Breakfast, a shower, a suspiciously long bathroom visit. Tiny passengers, busy crew, pets and cleaning robots all have places to be.

02 / GO WITH THE FLOW

One good turn deserves another.

Tap to rotate shared pipes and guide wastewater to treatment. Watch the next demand, switch the route, and come back before a puddle becomes a problem.

03 / ROOM TO EXPLORE

Small ship. Bigger adventures.

Five ship sizes. 12 to 144 rooms. Zoom in on the little stories, pull back to spot trouble, and discover a new layout every voyage.
